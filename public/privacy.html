Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

This website is operated by Anirudh Belwadi and is intended to showcase portfolio work and personal projects.

Information I Collect

When you visit this website, the following analytics information is collected automatically:

  • Your IP address
  • Approximate location based on your IP address (city, region, country, and postal code)
  • The network your IP address belongs to (for example, your internet provider, employer, or cloud host)
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Referring source (e.g., Google, LinkedIn, etc.)
  • Device type (mobile or desktop)
  • Your browser's user agent, which identifies the browser and operating system you use
  • Your browser's timezone and language settings
  • Whether your browser reports that it is being controlled by automation software

Some information is collected only if you choose to provide it:

  • Your name and role, if you answer the visitor prompt
  • Your name, email address, and message, if you use the contact form. Your IP address is sent along with your message.

How I Use Your Information

The data is used to analyze visitor trends, tell genuine visitors apart from automated traffic, reply to messages, and improve the content and performance of this website. Your information is never sold.

Cookies

This website uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, which set cookies to measure how visitors use the site. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings without affecting how the site works.

Data Storage

Analytics data and visitor prompt answers are stored in a database on this website's analytics server, hosted by PythonAnywhere. Contact form messages are delivered through Google Apps Script. The information is retained only for analytics purposes and for replying to messages.

Third-Party Services

This website relies on the following services, each of which receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • PythonAnywhere hosts the analytics server and its database.
  • ipify is contacted by your browser to look up your IP address.
  • IPinfo receives your IP address to determine its approximate location and network.
  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager measure site usage.
  • Google reCAPTCHA protects the contact form from spam. Its use is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Google Apps Script delivers contact form messages.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on this website.
  • Buy Me a Coffee serves the support button image in the footer.

Your Rights

If you are a visitor from the European Union (EU) or other jurisdictions with data protection regulations, you may request deletion of any data associated with your IP address, or of any information you submitted. Please email anirudh.belwadi@gmail.com to submit a request.

Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
